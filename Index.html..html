About SomaHub

Who we are

SomaHub is a learning platform for students and working adults who want practical skills without leaving home. Every course is split into short lessons that you can finish in about twenty minutes.

Register for a course, watch the lessons, practise with quizzes and join a live class once a week. Our tutors review your work and give feedback.
